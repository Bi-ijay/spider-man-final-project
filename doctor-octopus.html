Who Is Doctor Octopus?

Doctor Octopus, also known as Otto Octavius, is one of Spider-Man's most famous enemies. He is a brilliant scientist who becomes a villain after an accident causes four mechanical arms to become attached to his body.

Doctor Octopus uses his mechanical arms to fight Spider-Man, climb buildings, and lift heavy objects. His scientific knowledge and powerful mechanical arms make him a dangerous opponent.

Doctor Octopus's Abilities

  • Brilliant scientific intelligence
  • Four powerful mechanical arms
  • Superhuman-like strength using his mechanical arms
  • Can climb walls using his mechanical arms
  • Skilled at creating advanced technology
