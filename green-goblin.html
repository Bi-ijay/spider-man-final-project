Who Is Green Goblin?

The Green Goblin is one of Spider-Man's most famous and dangerous enemies. He is usually portrayed as Norman Osborn, a wealthy businessman and brilliant scientist who becomes the Green Goblin.

The Green Goblin uses advanced technology and weapons to fight Spider-Man. He is known for his Goblin Glider, pumpkin bombs, and other dangerous gadgets. His intelligence, strength, and unpredictable behavior make him a powerful villain.

Green Goblin's Abilities

  • Superhuman strength
  • Enhanced speed and agility
  • High intelligence
  • Uses a Goblin Glider
  • Uses pumpkin bombs and advanced weapons
